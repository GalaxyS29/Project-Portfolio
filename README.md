## Personal Projects  
**Vintage Sansui AU-517 Integrated Amp Restoration | May - June 2026**  
Solo Project  
Goal: to faithfully restore the Amplifier to its original, fully-working state.  
Replaced driver board and power board filter capacitors, balance potentiometer, ground-less AC power cord.  
Fixed no output on right channel, dialed in DC bias on both driver boards.  
Demonstrated intuitive diagnostic thinking and circuit schematic analysis.  
[Image: Deconstructed Sansui Amplifier](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HZG3i14cEDPQ61i9cSfHbvraVg5syg1t/view?usp=sharing)  

**Large Bookshelf Speakers | June - August 2026**  
Solo Project  
Goal: to design and building full-range (no subwoofer required) speakers with well-matched crossover points and extended high and low frequencies within a 1.5 cubic foot volume.
Designed and constructed speaker enclosures from MDF using power tools.  
Hand selected a 3-way driver system and designed a passive crossover from scratch with VituixCAD.  
Used audio measurement software and sound capturing techniques to gather driver-enclosure data.
